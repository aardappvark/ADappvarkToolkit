Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 4, 2026 | Last Updated: August 8, 2026 | Version 1.3.0

1. Introduction

AardAppvark Toolkit ("we," "our," "us," or the "Company") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App") and related services.

Our Core Privacy Principles

  1. Data Minimization: We collect only the absolute minimum data necessary to provide our Services.
  2. Local-First: We store data locally on your device whenever possible.
  3. Transparency: We are clear about what we collect and why.
  4. No Profiling: We use no advertising SDKs, no cross-app trackers and no profiling. The only telemetry is anonymous aggregate event counts (see section 2.2) that cannot be tied to you or your device.
  5. User Control: You maintain control over your data at all times.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Wallet Address: Your Solana wallet public address when you connect your wallet. Used for payment verification and transaction processing.
  • Consent Records: Timestamp of your acceptance of Terms of Service, stored locally for legal compliance.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data (for Sanctions Compliance): Approximate country determined from your SIM card country, network carrier country or device locale. The App does not request any Android location permission, so GPS is only ever used if you have already granted location access to the App for some other reason. Used solely to verify you are not in a blocked jurisdiction. Checked at app launch, every 30 minutes while the App is open, and before each payment transaction. Not stored after verification — only the result (allowed/blocked) is retained.
  • Device and Package Information: The App reads the list of applications installed on your device to show you what can be uninstalled or reinstalled. That list stays on your device — we never upload it. Two narrow exceptions, both anonymous: (a) if you tap Install on a Featured dApp card, the anonymous event showcase_tap:<that dApp's package name> is sent so the sponsor can be told how their placement performed, and a matching showcase_install event if that dApp appears installed within 7 days; (b) when the App looks up a dApp's icon it asks the Solana dApp Store catalogue about that package. Neither carries any identifier for you or your device.
  • Anonymous Aggregate Analytics: The App sends an event type only (for example app_open, wallet_connected, bulk_uninstall, payment_sol, geo_blocked) to our analytics endpoint at aardappvark-toolkit-analytics.aardappvark.workers.dev. That endpoint derives an approximate country and city from the network IP address of the request and stores only daily counts per country/city/event. The IP address itself is never stored, and no user ID, device ID, wallet address or advertising identifier is ever sent. We treat this as anonymous statistical data (GDPR Recital 26). Aggregate totals are published at the same host under /public-stats.
  • Uninstall History (on your device, in a folder you own): So your reinstall list survives the App itself being uninstalled, the App writes a copy of your uninstall history to Documents/AardAppvark/uninstall_history.json in your device's own Documents folder. It is not uploaded anywhere. You can delete it at any time from Settings → More → "Delete A Backup File", or with any file manager.
  • App Usage Information (Local Only): Credit balance, payment receipts (transaction signatures) and preferences. Stored locally.

2.3 Information We DO NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers (name, email or phone) — except the contact email you type yourself if you buy a Featured dApp placement (see section 9)
  • No Device Identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID)
  • No Private Keys or seed phrases (payments are signed inside your wallet, never by us)
  • No Crash Reports
  • No Advertising SDKs, cross-app trackers or behavioural profiling
  • No Photos, Contacts, or Media Access
  • No account: there is nothing to sign up for and no server-side profile

5. Third-Party Services and Network Endpoints

These are every host the App contacts, and what each one receives:

  • Solana RPC providers — mainnet.helius-rpc.com (Helius, primary) and, as fallbacks, api.mainnet-beta.solana.com (Solana Labs) and solana-rpc.publicnode.com (Allnodes). To verify Seeker Genesis Token status, submit payments and read token data, the App sends your wallet's public address to these providers. They receive your IP address and that public address. Private keys never leave your wallet.
  • TRM Labs sanctions screening — api.trmlabs.com. Before a payment, your wallet's public address is screened against sanctions lists using TRM Labs' free public screening API. TRM Labs receives that public address and your IP address. If the call fails, the App falls back to an offline list bundled in the App.
  • Our analytics endpoint — aardappvark-toolkit-analytics.aardappvark.workers.dev (Cloudflare Workers, run by us). Receives an event type and, at the network level, your IP address, from which an approximate country/city is derived and then discarded. Only aggregate counts are stored. See section 2.2.
  • Featured dApp feed — sponsr.provenby.dev and sponsr-kappa.vercel.app (Vercel, run by us). Serves the Featured dApp card's copy and images, and receives Featured-placement applications you choose to submit (section 10).
  • Solana dApp Store catalogue — dappstore.solanamobile.com (Solana Mobile). Queried for dApp names and icons. Receives the package name being looked up and your IP address.
  • IPFS / Arweave gateways — ipfs.io, arweave.net. Used only to fetch dApp icon images referenced by on-chain metadata.
  • Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter — on-device communication with your wallet app. No network service of ours is involved.
  • Solana blockchain — payments are public on-chain transactions by design.
  • Shizuku (optional, if you have it installed) — local, on-device privileged operations. No network use.

We do not sell or share any of the above with data brokers, advertising networks or analytics vendors.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • All of your App data is stored on your device — in the App's private storage, plus the uninstall-history file in your own Documents folder described in section 2.2.
  • Protected by Android's application sandboxing.
  • We operate no server that stores personal data. Our only backend is the analytics endpoint in section 5, which holds aggregate counts by country/city/day and nothing that identifies you.

6.1 Android Backup — what survives uninstalling

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, the App's settings and progress data are included in your device's encrypted Google backup, survive uninstalling the App, and may be restored when you reinstall. This is deliberate for one thing in particular: credits you have paid for are recorded only on your device, so excluding them from backup would mean losing them on reinstall. Caches that can be re-derived (Seeker Genesis Token status, dApp icons, the Featured feed) are excluded from backup. No wallet keys or seed material are stored by the App at all, so there is nothing of that kind in any backup. You can exclude the App from backup in Android Settings, or delete the backup from your Google account.

The uninstall-history file in Documents/AardAppvark/ is also, by design, not removed when you uninstall the App — that is what lets you restore your reinstall list afterwards. Delete it yourself if you do not want it kept.

7. Geographic Restrictions

The App is not available in 58 blocked jurisdictions including countries subject to comprehensive sanctions (Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Russia), significant restrictions (Belarus, Venezuela, Myanmar, Nicaragua, Eritrea), targeted sanctions, and FATF/AML high-risk designations. The complete list, with ISO country codes, is in our Terms of Service.

Your country is determined from your SIM card, network carrier and device locale (and GPS only if you have separately granted the App location access) at app launch, every 30 minutes while the App is open, and before each payment. If a blocked jurisdiction is detected the App is not usable. Payments are refused if your country cannot be determined at all.

8. Your Rights

  • Access and portability: Settings → Payments & your data → Export My Data writes everything the App holds about you (consent record, wallet link, Seeker status, credits, payment receipts, uninstall history) to a JSON file you choose.
  • Erasure: Settings → Payments & your data → Delete My Data erases all of it from the device. Note that this does not and cannot reverse an on-chain payment, and does not delete the uninstall-history file in your Documents folder (use Settings → More → "Delete A Backup File" for that) or any Google backup you have taken.
  • Analytics: the aggregate counts described in section 2.2 contain no personal data, so there is nothing to access, correct or erase — and consequently no opt-out toggle is offered. If you would rather send nothing at all, deny the App network access in Android Settings or uninstall it.
  • Complaints: contact us at the address below, or your local data protection authority.

We honour GDPR and CCPA/CPRA rights to the extent they apply to the very limited data described above. We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA, and we do not process personal data of children.

9. Sponsored "Featured dApp" Placements

The Home screen can show a Featured dApp card. Some placements are our own apps (marked as ours) and some are paid placements bought by other developers through Settings → Get Featured.

If you buy a placement, the App sends the details you type into that form — app name, package name, tagline, website and your contact email — together with your payment's transaction signature to sponsr.provenby.dev, and keeps a copy on your device as your receipt. We use that email only to contact you about your placement. Placements are reviewed and scheduled manually, so paying does not publish a listing instantly.

If you merely see or tap a Featured card, the only data sent is the anonymous event described in section 2.2. There is no advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking and no ad network involved.
