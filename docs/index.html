What it does

AardAppvark Toolkit is the essential dApp lifecycle manager built exclusively for Solana Seeker. Bulk uninstall multiple dApps in seconds instead of removing them one at a time, then batch reinstall previously removed apps straight from the dApp Store with Auto-Accept mode. Connect your Solana wallet to unlock verified Seeker Genesis Token features including bonus credits, unlimited auto-accept, and a detailed analytics dashboard tracking your installed dApps, storage usage, and favourites.

Features

🗑️

Bulk Uninstall

Remove multiple dApps in seconds with hands-free auto-confirm of system dialogs.

📥

Batch Reinstall

Reinstall previously removed dApps in one tap straight from the Solana dApp Store.

⚡

Auto-Accept

Skip system dialogs during batch operations using Android's accessibility service.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Track installed dApps, storage usage, favourites, and usage patterns at a glance.

🔐

Seeker Genesis Token

Connect your wallet and verify SGT to unlock unlimited auto-accept and bonus credits.

⭐

Favourites & Sorting

Mark dApps as protected favourites so they're never accidentally uninstalled.

Install

Two ways to install:

  1. Solana dApp Store (recommended) — search for AardAppvark Toolkit on your Seeker, then tap Install.
  2. Solana dApp Store — open the dApp Store on your Seeker and search for AardAppvark Toolkit, or use the button at the top of this page. Updates arrive through the store automatically.
